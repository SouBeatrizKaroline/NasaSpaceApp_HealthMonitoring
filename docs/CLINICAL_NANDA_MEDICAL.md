# Clinical Information Model and Professional Review

This document replaces earlier claims that embedded diagnosis catalogs, codes, or care recommendations. The current prototype collects self-reported information and does not diagnose.

## Generic anamnesis fields

- Primary reason for check-in and what changed from the person's baseline.
- Symptom onset, duration, location, pattern, severity as experienced, and associated symptoms.
- Recent mission phase, gravity transition, workload, sleep, exercise, and relevant environmental context.
- Effects on mobility, balance, vision-related tasks, exercise, eating, sleep, work, and daily activities.
- Optional measurement with value, unit, method, timestamp, and reliability note.
- Follow-up preference and an optional request for a professional review.

## Professional domains

Future human review could route to an authorized clinician or professional in flight medicine, nursing, psychology or psychiatry, physical therapy, occupational therapy, nutrition, pharmacy, or environmental health. Routing depends on approved mission roles and consent.

## Classification safeguards

NANDA-I terminology is licensed. WHO classification content has specific copyright and translation conditions. ICF offers a useful function-and-context framework but is not itself a physiotherapy diagnosis. CBDF is a Brazil-specific reference, not a universal mission standard. No codes, labels, diagnostic criteria, or treatment protocols are shipped in this demo.

## Clinical status

Workflow labels are administrative prompts. Only authorized mission procedures and qualified professionals may interpret observations, set thresholds, assign diagnoses, or recommend treatment.
